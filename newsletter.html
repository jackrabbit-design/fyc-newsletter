From the Bridge

A Note from the Commodore

 

Dear Members,

Welcome to the first edition of our new Club newsletter! Each week, this is where you’ll find what’s happening around the Club: upcoming events, dining specials, member gatherings, and a few familiar faces along the way. We hope it makes it a little easier to stay connected, whether you’re here every week or following along from afar.

With the holidays just around the corner, there’s a lot to look forward to. We’re especially excited for Friendsgiving on Saturday, November 14, a chance to gather with old friends and new ones before the busy season sets in. We hope to see you there, and at the bar on Thursdays for Burger Night.

Warm regards,

Jamie Tormey

Commodore

Special Event · Saturday, November 14 · 5:30 PM

Friendsgiving

 

Gather with fellow members for an elegant evening of friendship, gratitude, and exceptional cuisine! Enjoy cocktails and hors d’oeuvres followed by Chef Franco’s seasonal three-course dinner, featuring your choice of braised short rib or halibut.

$65++ per person  ·  Ages 21+

Only 20 spots remaining!

Please note: there will be no regular dining or bar service that evening.

Please RSVP with your entrée selection through the FYC App or fb@falmouthyc.com.

Guests are encouraged to bring a nonperishable donation to support the Falmouth Service Center. Donations are being collected through November 22.
